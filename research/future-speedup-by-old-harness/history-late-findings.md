## 1. Main findings (read this first)

F1. Approval and question gating is the owner's most intense and most repeated complaint (2026-10-12 .. 10-16), and its cause was a rule applied literally, not a missing instruction. Raw proof in my partition: 14 calls of the ask tool, 10 were "Ask tool was cancelled by the user", 8 of the 11 start-type gates ("Начинаем?") were cancelled. The agent itself admitted the mechanism (01a12e78-acfa:L110: main-workflow demanded one ask before small/large work, and it treated a research question as the start of work). Owner's final rule (01a130bc-c3a6:L3964, 01a133b8-fc93:L5987): ask only about real ambiguity and about decisions the agent invented itself; if the plan is unambiguous, start at once. This rule is already in skills/main-workflow (commit f30ff84); SYSTEM.md carries no matching base sentence.

F2. Two opposite intent failures coexist. (a) Over-action on discussion/questions (01a12e1a-ed6b:L68, 01a12e78-acfa:L88/L109, 01a12ddd-b530:L178, 01a12f57-7bd3:L78, 01a12e27-ae79:L170). (b) Under-action on defect reports and instructions: stopped at a diagnosis (01a14556-ee67:L206-L211), or ended a turn with an offer/question about an already authorized next step (01a13629-d2f7:L201-L202, 01a12e27-ae79:L330-L331). The discriminator in the data is the owner's explicit framing: he announces discussion mode ("пока ничего не планируй", "не запускай механизм ask", "это вопрос был"); absent that, a statement of expected behaviour or a defect plus "перепроверь/делай" means fix it.

F3. Losing the thread across compaction is the owner's main stated quality problem, and he built a mechanism for it. 01a130bc-c3a6 ran 7 compactions (5 opaque GPT remote ones whose summary field is only 850-1050 characters: a header line plus a list of files read), after at least two of them the agent argued against facts it had established and drifted (L2415, L2419, L4516). The owner opened 01a133b8-fc93 to diagnose it; the agent's own diagnosis (L42): it lost the anchor on the source of truth and replaced reproduction with its own decisions presented as compliance. Result: the decisions registry extension (extensions/decisions.ts, entries my-omp-harness.decisions). Owner's model of the target behaviour: later statements cumulatively supersede earlier ones, cancelled ones stay marked, the agent keeps the final decision with its justification.

F4. Steering is the normal interaction mode. 85 steering messages and 84 aborted assistant turns in 504 non-synthetic owner messages. The steering messages are overwhelmingly additions, narrowings or corrections of the running task (example chain 01a130bc-c3a6:L367 -> L373 -> L376 -> L379: push -> only own branch -> no finalize -> no checks; the agent correctly replaced the release plan with a branch push). Cancellations are rare and explicit (01a141ad-07c9:L714 "Отменяй, сворачивай, удаляй… Откатывай"; 01a13bd2-e2bf:L72 "Предыдущая сдача отменяется"). A bare "продолжай/доделай" follows an aborted turn in 11 of 12 cases and means resume the same work.

F5. Check economy: owner wants checks proportional to the change and aimed at the diff, and he calls over-checking out; but explicit orders for heavy checks are frequent and always task-specific, and the order beats the default. His own recurring acceptance phrase is "nothing lost, nothing extra". Measured example (agent-computed): a report-only task carried ~1 min of workflow overhead before work and 18 min after (15 smarty rounds that removed 10k characters and added nothing) (01a130bc-c3a6:L3670).
