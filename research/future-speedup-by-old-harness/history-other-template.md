# HistoryOther findings: owner workflow evidence from 270 non-main-project OMP sessions

Author: HistoryOther (research only; no project edits). Source partition: `local://omp-history-other.jsonl` (270 session records; raw transcripts under `/Users/arkadijcukavin/.omp/agent/sessions/<project>/<ts>_<id>.jsonl`). Evidence = raw JSONL user messages plus neighbouring assistant/tool records. Summaries, recaps and `sol-workflow-analysis.md` were used only for navigation. All Russian quotes below are extracted programmatically from the JSONL text and verified verbatim (profanity retained as written). Reference format `[S<n>:L<line>]` = session index n in Appendix A, JSONL line number; Appendix B maps each cited reference to record id, timestamp and file path.

Caution on scope of the evidence: the main agent in these sessions was mostly Claude Opus 5.5 / Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol (per-session model table in Appendix A notes), not the GPT-5.5-based prompt under review. The findings are about the owner's preferences and about failure patterns that appeared across models; whether a given prompt wording prevents a failure is a hypothesis, not a measurement. No secrets are reproduced (two sessions contain pasted API tokens; they are deliberately not quoted).

## 0. Headline findings (most durable first)

1. **Result minimal, path fast.** The owner explicitly separates the size of the deliverable (minimal, no extras) from the way to get there (fast, wide, parallel, few turns, low TPS). This is stated once at length in [S8:L162] and repeated in different forms from 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-16. Strongly durable.
2. **Exact order, no substitution.** Largest cluster of corrections: agent did a different, wider or narrower thing than ordered (wrong repo, wrong metric, partial application of an "all" instruction, unrequested report, abridged text reported as verbatim). Strongly durable.
3. **Discussion/question versus order.** Repeated misparse in both directions: clarifying questions treated as orders (agent edited), and declarative sentences that were check-questions treated as statements. Moderately durable (seen 2026-10-04..10-05 and again in dictated form 2026-10-15).
4. **Goal continuity is mostly interrupted by the environment, not by the model quietly giving up.** Of 63 short "продолжай / какой статус / что там" pings, 30 followed an owner interrupt, 4 followed an API error, 16 were mid-run status requests and only 13 followed a turn the agent finished on its own. The real model-side gaps: long silent work or waiting without a status, and turns that end while background work is pending so the goal pauses.
5. **Done means working and shipped.** The owner's completion test is the real behaviour (not a plan, not a unit probe), executed by the agent itself, and for harness work includes release, commit and push; he asks "ты сделал релиз commit и push, да?" at the end of almost every harness task.
6. **Autonomy is bounded by ambiguity, not by risk labels.** He wants the agent to solve problems, test, deploy and clean up its own leftovers without asking; he wants a question only when a reference is ambiguous enough to send work to the wrong place.
7. **Brevity is the most frequent complaint** (44 distinct messages under a broad pattern, 26 under a strict one, 2026-10-04..10-17), peaking 10-05 and 10-07, falling afterwards.
8. **Delegation is owner-directed and model-family bound** (no cross-family subagents; explicit counts and tiers per task; free choice of agent type on request).
9. **Independent review is wanted for substantive work, chosen by the owner, one reviewer per stage**; he objects to reviewer chains and to silence while reviews run, not to review itself.
10. **Agreements are lost across compaction and on forks of work**; the owner designed a decisions registry for it (the harness now emits `my-omp-harness.decisions` records).

- Harness-generated text sitting in user or developer role (not owner preferences): ToSpec "Execute the approved ToSpec spec..." (S52, S71 L7, S75 L8), 20 "Plan approved" developer messages, 4 compaction resume injections ("Resume the user's latest intent. Episode (S8, 2026-10-05): ordered an "aggressive context gathering" section appended to the Codex prompt without touching the original ({{Q 8 15 | ты не трогаешь никак весь изначальный промпт}}). The agent added the section plus an override sentence because the original said "Minimize actions, calls, ... and elapsed work" and "Group independent reads and searches when useful" (agent text L161: "Taken literally, every extra read is a violation"). Owner corrected the premise: the minimization rules were written against GPT-5.6 Sol over-engineering (tests, fixtures, benchmarks) and may be making other models "тупить и медленно работать". The agent then formulated two axes (minimal result / fastest path) [S8:L163, agent's own wording] and proposed turn-count rules (L165); the owner did not reject it and moved to Cursor-style wide globs and greps (L186, L217) and later approved "более чем логично" the agent's reasoning on one question (L420).

Durability: strong, rule-level, multi-week. Contrary/later: the owner is not against watching production: {{Q 75 7329 | и последить за ним в течение хотя бы часа}} [S75:L7329, 10-11] and he asked for a one-hour watch on 10-07 [S38:L2349] before cancelling it [S38:L3058, L3606]; effort level is tuned per role by measurement (max workers were "хуета").
Implication (hypothesis): any base-prompt wording that frames "minimize calls/actions" must say it applies to the deliverable and side artefacts, not to reads, searches and parallel calls; per-turn cost matters more than per-call cost.
